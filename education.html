Oola Kenneth — An Education Journey

Every builder's story starts somewhere long before the first line of code. For Kenneth, that beginning was primary school — the years where foundational literacy, numeracy, and curiosity are first shaped. It's rarely dramatic at the time, but it's where the basic discipline of learning gets built: showing up, following through, and starting to notice what subjects pull your attention more than others.

Secondary school is usually where those pulls sharpen into direction. Somewhere along that stretch, Kenneth's interest in technology and systems-thinking likely took root — the kind of inclination that later shows up as a preference for structure, logic, and how things connect. It's often in these years that a student starts choosing science and technical subjects not because they're required, but because they make sense in a way other subjects don't.

That inclination carried him to Uganda Christian University, where he's now pursuing a Diploma in Information Technology under the Faculty of Engineering, Design and Technology at the Mukono campus. University is where the general curiosity from earlier years turns into applied skill — and for Kenneth, that's shown up clearly: computer networking, full-stack software development (React, Express.js, MySQL/MariaDB), database design, and technical documentation have all become areas of real, hands-on competence rather than just subjects on a syllabus.

The clearest evidence of that shift from "learning" to "building" is ReConnect, his UCU Lost & Found Portal — an Agile-run bootcamp project addressing a genuine campus need. It's the kind of project that marks a turning point in an education journey: no longer just completing assignments, but producing something real, with real users in mind.

And the journey isn't finished. Kenneth is now extending his skills from web development into mobile, using React Native — treating his diploma not as a finish line, but as a foundation for a much longer career in software development.
